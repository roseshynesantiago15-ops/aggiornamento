THE FILM

Faith in a changing world

Aggiornamento takes its name from the Italian word for “bringing up to date.” The student film project looks at what that idea means for the Church, from the Second Vatican Council to a world of livestreams and social media.

The poster brings those settings together. Church architecture and prayer appear alongside phones and social feeds, reflecting how the places where people encounter faith have expanded.

HISTORICAL BACKGROUND

Vatican II & the Modern Church

1963

Inter mirifica

The Council recognized that media such as film, radio, and television could help share the Gospel when used responsibly.

1963

Sacrosanctum concilium

The Council called for the full, conscious, and active participation of the faithful in the liturgy.

1965

Gaudium et spes

The Council asked the Church to engage with contemporary society and interpret the changes of its time in the light of the Gospel.

Reflections

Draft one-liners for the group to personalize.

  • The church beside the phone on our poster makes me think about how Inter mirifica opened up new ways to share the Gospel.
  • Vatican II called for active participation in worship, which makes me ask how our parish can help people feel more involved today.
  • Gaudium et spes reminds me that listening to what people face today is part of how the Church lives out its mission.
  • A parish livestream can help someone at home stay connected to a community they already know and love.
  • The social feeds in our poster made me think about the responsibility that comes with sharing a message of faith online.
  • Showing prayer alongside social media helped me understand how the Church can speak to a new generation through familiar tools.
